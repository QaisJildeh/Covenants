# V7a — Verification of people's dates in Morrow, *The Covenants of the Prophet Muhammad with the Christians of the World* (2013)

Input list: `people/people_1a.md` (155 rows; rows numbered 1–155 in file order). Checked 10 Nov 2026.

**Method.** Every verdict rests on pages that were opened and read, not on search snippets. The URL of each page is in the last column. The sources used:

- **Authority files:** GND (Deutsche Nationalbibliothek), read as lobid.org JSON (`https://lobid.org/gnd/<id>.json`, the same record as `https://d-nb.info/gnd/<id>`), and BnF catalogue authority records.
- **Encyclopaedias:** Encyclopaedia of Islam 2nd ed. (Brill abstract pages on referenceworks.brill.com), TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi (a peer-reviewed Turkish encyclopaedia of Islam), Encyclopaedia Iranica, the Catholic Encyclopedia (newadvent.org), Treccani DBI, the old DNB on Wikisource, Magyar életrajzi lexikon, Russkij biograficheskij slovar'.
- **Primary texts:** digitised on archive.org or Gutenberg.
- **Wikidata entity JSON:** each claim is cited together with its own source.

Wikipedia was used only for: the Volney travel dates, the summary of the scholarly debate on Vegetius, and confirming that Bangash's date has no source. Each of these is labelled in its row.

**Access limits.** Britannica, Oxford DNB, Library of Congress (id.loc.gov) and VIAF all refused automated access (403/502/404), so LoC/ODNB data appear only where Wikidata cites them. The shared web-search budget ran out near the end.

**Who checked what.** Rows 10, 19, 32–33 (travels), 36, 41, 43–45 (travels), 47–51, 58, 60, 62–65, 68, 71–73, 75, 78, 87–89, 99, 104, 119, 122, 126, 129, 139–141, 143, 146–148, 151, 155 were researched by two helper agents, who opened the cited pages. Their key claims were spot-checked by re-opening:

- BnF on Safdar Husayn
- GND on Musavi Lari
- Iranica on Petrus de Monte, Pacifique de Provins and A. ʿAlawi
- Stanley's *Sinai and Palestine*
- Arundale's *Illustrations*

All spot-checks confirmed the helpers' readings.

**AH→CE.** A Hijri year spans two Gregorian years. A one-year difference that comes from that overlap is graded "Minor". Garbled OCR in the printed dates (e.g. "16161689", "QE", "Vegeitus", "1951CE") was read in the obvious way and the dates checked. No row needed the separate verdict "OCR issue".

**Deaths after publication (not errors).** Patricia Crone d. 2015; F. E. Peters d. 2020; John L. Esposito d. 15 Jul 2026 (GND/BnF).

## 2. Inconsistent and Disputed rows (most significant first)

**Inconsistent (12 rows)**

1. **Row 97 — 'Abd Allah ibn Sallam, "d. 630".** EI2 and TDV both give 43/663-4, and BnF gives 663. The book is 33 years early. EI2 also records his activity under 'Umar and 'Uthman, which rules out a death in 630.
2. **Row 135 — "George Jordac or Jurji Zaydan (1861–1914)".** These are two different men. 1861–1914 are Jurji Zaydan's dates (EI2, TDV). The quoted work on 'Ali is by George Jordac (Jurj Jurdaq), 1931–2014 (BnF, GND).
3. **Row 102 — "Muhammad al-Kalbi (737–819)".** Muhammad b. al-Sa'ib al-Kalbi died 146/763 (TDV). 737–819 belong to his son Hisham Ibn al-Kalbi (BnF, TDV). The book's own source (Schöller) is about Muhammad.
4. **Row 13 — al-Majlisi, "1616–1689" (pp. 2, 6, 7).** Iranica gives b. 1627, d. 1699 or 1700. GND gives 1627–1698 and TDV d. 1110/1698-99. Both ends are about 11 years early.
5. **Row 51 — Sayyed Safdar Husayn, "1932–1989".** The book cites his *Histoire des premiers temps de l'Islam* (1991). BnF's record for that author is 1868–1949, with an explicit warning not to confuse him with Safdar Husain Najafi. The book has mixed up two men.
6. **Row 152 — Ja'far al-Sadiq, "(711–36)"** (Arpee's figures, repeated by the book). He was born 83/702 and died 148/765, and his imamate ran from 117/735 or 114/732 (Iranica, TDV, BnF). "711–36" fits neither his life nor his imamate.
7. **Row 58 — A. P. Stanley "visited Saint Catherine's in 1858".** Stanley's own *Sinai and Palestine* (1856) puts the visit in winter 1852 and spring 1853 (at Feiran on 9 Mar 1853). The DNB agrees. A book published in 1856 cannot describe an 1858 visit.
8. **Row 54 — "Abu 'Ali al-Mansur (r. 996–1021), the tenth of the Fatimid Caliphs".** The reign is al-Hakim's, but al-Hakim was the 6th Fatimid caliph. The 10th was al-Amir (1101–1130), who had the same name, Abu 'Ali al-Mansur (TDV). The book has merged the two.
9. **Row 41 — Musavi Lari, "1925–2013".** GND gives b. 1935. WikiShia gives b. 1314 SH (= 1935/36) and d. 9 Mar 2013. The 1925 probably comes from al-islam.org's mis-conversion "1314/1925".
10. **Row 59 — 'Amr ibn al-'As, "592–664".** EI2 says he was "over ninety" at his death (c. 42/663), so he was born before c. 573. GND gives c. 570 or c. 575. The death year 664 is fine (TDV 43/664).
11. **Row 114 — Abu Hurayrah, "603–681".** EI2 gives d. 57, 58 or 59 AH (probably 58/678 or 59), aged 78. TDV gives 58/678. GND gives 602–679 and BnF 601?–677?. No source checked gives 681.
12. **Row 50 — Francis Arundale "visited ... in 1831" (pp. 11, 21).** He went to Egypt in 1831 (DNB). His own *Illustrations* show the caravan leaving Cairo for Sinai on 29 Aug 1833.

**Disputed among sources (9 rows)**

13. **Row 106 — 'Umar ibn al-Khattab b. 579.** Sources give GND 568, BnF 581, TDV c. 583 and Britannica (via Wikidata) c. 586. No source checked gives 579. The death year 644 is consistent.
14. **Row 96 — Salman al-Farisi d. 654.** TDV says end of 35 or start of 36 AH (656) and notes other dates. Islamskiy ents. slovar' gives 657. No source checked gives 654.

18. **Row 8 — Hermann of Carinthia c. 1100–c. 1160.** BnF gives 1110–1154. Other records give a birth of c. 1100 or 1110 and a death of c. 1143 or c. 1160.
19. **Row 148 — Ahmad 'Alawi 'Amili d. 1644.** Iranica: "d. between 1054/1644 and 1060/1650". 1644 is only the earliest possible year.
20. **Row 108 — Vegetius, "end of the 4th century".** This is one side of a scholarly dispute: dedication to Theodosius I (379–395) or Valentinian III (430s). BnF/GND give 383?–450?.
21. **Row 69 — St Catherine c. 282–c. 305.** She is a legendary figure (GND: "Fiktive Gestalt"). The Catholic Encyclopedia gives no years. The dates are traditional and appear only in Wikipedias.
