## 1. Appendix I: "Witnesses to the Covenants" (pp. 377–381 [PDF 402–406])

**Running heads.** Page 377 carries the chapter heading "Appendix 1 / Witnesses to the Covenants". Pages 379 and 381 have the running head "*Appendix 1*". Pages 378 and 380 have "THE COVENANTS OF THE PROPHET MUHAMMAD". The table of contents (front matter) calls it "Appendix I: Witnesses to the Covenants • 377".

**Structure.** This is not one table. It is four separate tables (A–D below), each with its own header row. Every table ends with a RELIABILITY column. Rows are not numbered in the book; the "#" column is my own numbering. Wherever a column has no name in a row, the book prints the word *Missing* in italics.

**Key / legend.** **No key or legend is printed anywhere in Appendix I (pp. 377–381).** The abbreviations "(S)" and "(SH)" are never expanded on these pages, and neither are the terms "Unknown" and "Missing".
- [Context from outside this section, offered as a pointer only: Chapter 13, "Examining the Authorities" (p. 323 [PDF 348]), discusses each witness's standing "by Sunnis" and "by Shi‘ites". That is consistent with S = Sunni and SH = Shi‘ite, but the appendix itself never says so.]
- "Unknown" stands alone in the RELIABILITY column, with no (S)/(SH) split.

## 2. Appendix II: "Possible Modes of Transmission of the Covenants" (pp. 382–383 [PDF 407–408])

**Headings.** Page 382 is headed "Appendix 2 / Possible Modes of Transmission of the Covenants"; p. 383 has the running head "*Appendix 2*". The table of contents (front matter) calls it "Appendix II … • 382". The appendix runs only pp. 382–383. Page 384 [PDF 409] is illustration 3 (see §3).

**Format.** The four "tables" are arrow diagrams (↓ and →). The captions of Tables 1–3 end in "(above)" and sit *below* their diagrams. Table 4's caption is printed at the top of p. 383, but its diagram is at the foot of p. 382.

### Table 1: "Multiple Individual Prophetic Transmissions (above)" (p. 382 [PDF 407])
- **Layout:** "Prophet Muhammad" at the top, a horizontal bar, then seven separate vertical arrow-columns. Left to right:
  1. **Najran** → (9th c. CE)
  2. **Sinai** → (1561 CE)
  3. **Assyria** → (1822 or 1845 CE)
  4. **Egypt** → (1538 CE)
  5. **Levant** → (Paris 1630)
  6. **Persia** → (17th c. CE)

- **Claim:** each covenant comes *independently and directly* from the Prophet. No covenant derives from another.
