Covenants Evidence Ledger
=========================
Supporting record for the report "Book Analysis Report: Timeline, Facts & Cross-Source Verification"
on John Andrew Morrow, The Covenants of the Prophet Muhammad with the Christians of the World (2013).

Covenants_Evidence_Ledger.html   Open in a browser. Every extracted item and every fact-check verdict,
                                 searchable, with a filter by verdict (Inconsistent, Disputed, ...).
book_extraction/                 What the book says, section by section, with printed page and PDF page.
  00_master_timeline.csv/.md     All 1,336 dated items merged and sorted by year.
fact_check_reports/              One report per topic. Each row: the book's claim (page), verdict,
                                 what independent sources say, and the URLs opened.
  QA_extraction_checks.md        Script checks of the extraction against the PDF text.
  AUDIT_A.md, AUDIT_B.md         Independent re-check of 26 key findings.
  FINAL_REVIEW_of_report.md      Review of the report against all evidence files (its fixes are applied).
  count_verdicts_final.py        Reproduces the verdict counts in the report.

Page references: printed page first, PDF page in brackets. The PDF repeats printed pages 32-33 (PDF 55-58).
